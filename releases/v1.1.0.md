# HanTalk AI v1.1.0 — Voice-first release

**Release date:** 2026-11-16
**Version:** `1.1.0` (versionCode 2)
**APK:** `HanTalkAI-1.1.0-release.apk`
**Minimum Android:** 8.0 (API 26)

---

## The headline

The app now **opens in voice mode**. Instead of landing on a text chat, you land
on one big talk button: tap it, speak Korean (or English), and the tutor answers
out loud in Korean. A total beginner can hold a first conversation without
knowing 한글 yet — and the rest of the app is still one tap away.

This release is also a full UI rebuild: the stock-Material look is gone.

---

## New: voice mode

| | |
|---|---|
| **Speak** | Tap the orb → Korean speech recognition (Korean or English prompt) |
| **Listen** | The tutor's answer is read back with Korean text-to-speech |
| **Replay** | Replay the answer, or play it at 0.7× for pronunciation practice |
| **SAY THIS** | One fresh phrase per visit · *Hear it* / *Slowly* / *Practise* |
| **Mute** | One-tap auto-speak toggle in the top bar |
| **Fallback** | No speech service or Korean TTS voice? The screen says so plainly and typing still works |

Details worth knowing:

- The voice orb is fully procedural (no image assets): a gradient core, two
  counter-rotating rings, a radial halo and 44 audio bars that react to the
  microphone level, and it changes colour per state — indigo when idle or
  listening, amber while thinking, jade while speaking.
- Markdown, tables and romanisation are stripped before speaking, so TTS only
  pronounces actual Korean instead of reading out `**asterisk asterisk**`.
- Speech is started through a permission launcher, so the first tap explains
  itself instead of failing silently.

## New: design system

- **Palette** — haneul indigo brand gradient, jade/coral/amber semantic accents,
  warm *한지 paper* surfaces. Full **light and dark** themes, including matching
  status-bar icon contrast.
- **Surfaces** — hairline borders and 20–32 dp radii instead of grey tonal cards;
  no drop shadows, so dark mode and low-end screens stay clean.
- **Type** — 8-step scale with tighter tracking on display sizes and a
  comfortable 25-sp body line height.
- **Components** — `SoftCard`, `Pill` / `GhostPill`, `StatStrip`, `ScoreBadge`,
  gradient progress `Bar`, redesigned `PhraseRow` and `ScreenHeader`.
- **Navigation** — custom bottom bar; the selected destination gets a gradient
  pill (no stock `NavigationBar` indicator).
- **Screens** — Tutor, Lessons, Alphabet, Practice and AI models all rebuilt with
  hero cards and audio controls; letter tiles show learned state, and practice
  results animate in with a score badge.

## Improved

- **Tutor chat** — avatar per reply, animated "thinking" dots, a *Read aloud*
  action under every answer, and the composer/header cleaned up.
- **Lessons** — phrase of the day is now a gradient hero with *Hear it* and
  *Practise*; every phrase row has a speaker button; the detail sheet plays audio
  at normal and slow speed.
- **Alphabet** — progress bar with percentage, tiles mark themselves learned,
  and the letter detail card plays the letter and shows it inside a real syllable
  block.
- **Practice** — you can now answer **by voice** as well as by typing; results
  get a score badge and slow playback of the correct answer.
- **AI models** — an "active tutor" card, clearer RAM/size pills, and a download
  button that explains itself when a model is too large for the device.

## Fixed

- **Speech services were invisible on Android 11+.** Added package-visibility
  `<queries>` for `RecognitionService` and `TTS_SERVICE`; without them
  `isRecognitionAvailable()` returns false even when the services are installed.
- **Empty answers.** If the on-device model streams nothing, the chat no longer
  leaves an empty bubble — it says so and suggests rephrasing.
- **First utterance was dropped.** `KoreanSpeaker` now queues the first speak
  request until the engine has finished starting.
- **Edge-to-edge bars.** Status and navigation bars are transparent and get the
  correct light/dark icon treatment.
- **Launcher icon** refreshed to the new brand colour.

## Under the hood

- `speech/VoiceRecognizer.kt` — new Korean ASR wrapper: partial results, RMS
  level metering, readable errors for no-match / no-permission / busy / offline,
  and a clean `Unavailable` state.
- `speech/KoreanSpeaker.kt` — exposes `isSpeaking`, adds `speakSlowly()`, and
  queues pending speech.
- `HanTalkViewModel` — `spoken` chat messages, `speakableReply()` extraction,
  phrase-of-the-moment state, and an auto-speak preference.
- `ui/theme/` — new `Color.kt`, `Theme.kt` (gradients + semantic tokens via
  composition locals), `Type.kt`, `Shape.kt`.
- Verified in CI: Kotlin compile, **31 unit tests**, signed debug + release APKs.

## Install

1. Download `HanTalkAI-1.1.0-release.apk` below.
2. Open it on your phone and allow "install unknown apps" if prompted.
3. Android 8.0 or newer.

> CI signs each build with a throwaway key, so this build is **not
> upgrade-compatible** with 1.0.0 — uninstall the old app first. Your streak and
> learned letters reset when you do; phrases and models are unaffected.

Model files (Gemma / Qwen) are *not* bundled — the rule-based offline tutor works
immediately, and you can install a model in-app for free-form conversation.
Nothing is uploaded anywhere; inference stays on the phone.

## Known limitations

- Voice input and Korean TTS depend on the device's speech services; a phone with
  no Korean TTS voice will stay silent and show a hint.
- Recognition quality depends on the installed speech engine and the network
  policy of that engine (some engines are online-only).
